Paw Path

Pawpath is an app designed to make dog walking smarter, safer, and more social. It helps dog owners discover personalized walking routes based on distance, terrain, and real-time updates like blocked roads or crowded areas. Beyond navigation, Pawpath also highlights nearby dog-friendly cafés, parks and water stations. It not only improves local everyday walks but also offers route recommendations for holidays.

The app also includes strong community features, allowing users to join group walks, organize local dog meetups, chat in-app, and build profiles for themselves and their pets. With Pawpath, dog owners can turn everyday walks into connected, engaging experiences for both humans and their furry companions.
