About Us

We are Canova – a brand trying to make the lives of people and their pets more social and adventurous!!

Our mission is to turn everyday routines, like walking your dog, into experiences that are easier, more social, unique and full of discovery.

Our Vision

Imagine a world where every walk is an adventure, every outing is an opportunity, and people feel connected to the other members of their vibrant, welcoming community.

Our Values

Make Everyday an Adventure
No day should be the same. Canova helps you shape new experiences.
Find Your People
Pet ownership is more fun when it’s a shared experience. We create opportunities for owners to connect.
Outdoorsiness
Our planet has endless offerings of beauty that should be enjoyed. Go touch some grass.
Sharing is Caring
Canova allows a community to share their tips and resources with each other. Who better to take advice from than someone who relates to your issues?
